# GentlePath Visual Assets

The October 28, 2026 iOS reference images guide the web presentation. They are visual evidence; embedded interface copy and pictured product features do not create new backend or workflow requirements. The website uses responsive web components rather than screenshots of phones.

Three original illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and copied into the repository. Existing source illustrations were preserved. Runtime components use Next Image for responsive delivery. All visible copy and alternative text remain in `src/strings.ts`.

## Generation Prompts

### Path Landscape

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide high-resolution botanical landscape illustration for the GentlePath family support website, 1536x1024 landscape composition. Primary request: a beautiful reassuring winding ivory walking path through layered rolling sage green hills, deep teal forest foliage and delicate hand-painted eucalyptus leaves in the foreground, a generous warm golden sun with simple fine radiating rays in the upper right, small soft ivory clouds, and one tiny lilac butterfly. Style: premium watercolor and cut-paper children's picture-book editorial illustration, subtle paper texture with layered translucent washes, refined organic curves, crisp silhouette edges and delicate leaf veins, sophisticated grown-up wellness brand rather than cartoon. Color palette: warm ivory, richly colored muted evergreen and deep teal, lively soft sage, gentle lavender, sunny honey yellow. Composition: landscape scene fills the lower two-thirds, generous airy pale sage/ivory sky in the top third, winding path goes from the lower center toward sunlit hills at right. Lush, heartwarming, luminous, visibly hand-crafted. No text, no typography, no letters, no logo, no UI, no phones, no frame, no border, no photorealism, no people. Full bleed opaque image.

### Reading Garden

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: premium botanical editorial illustration for GentlePath web resource cards and journal banners, wide landscape 1536x1024. Create a simple charming cut-paper watercolor still life: an open ivory book beside a small cream spiral notebook, nestled among oversized stylized sage and deep teal leaves, a little lavender butterfly hovering, a small flat golden sun with twelve simple rays. Backdrop soft lilac and sage gently overlapping abstract hill shapes on warm ivory paper. Clearly simplified geometric organic picture-book shapes, sparse large leaves with fine dark veins, soft watercolor-paper grain and restrained shading. Match a sophisticated caring family app illustration: warm, hopeful, clean silhouettes, tactile layered paper, no photorealism, no 3D plastic. Use only evergreen teal, sage, ivory, lilac and honey yellow. Composition concentrates book and notebook on the lower right two thirds, airy pale lilac/ivory negative space at top and left. Full bleed opaque illustration, no lettering or writing on pages, no text, no logos, no UI, no phone, no border. Illustrate objects only.
